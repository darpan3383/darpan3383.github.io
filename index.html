About

I'm Darpan — based in Nagpur, Maharashtra. I write across genres, but the through-lines are constant: the land and people of Vidarbha, a Christian faith that runs through the work, and a stubborn interest in the voices history left out.

The children's series began with two real animals — Oscar, a mostly-white tabby, and Leo, a black pariah dog — who now fight crime in print.
